Our Story

Flowers are our
language of love

Born from a passion for natural beauty, Bloom & Petal was founded in the heart of London to bring handcrafted, seasonal florals to every celebration — from intimate dinners to grand weddings.

Each arrangement is composed with intention, using only ethically sourced blooms from local growers and certified sustainable farms.
